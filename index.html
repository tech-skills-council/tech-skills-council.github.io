01 — The gap

Access without a cohort is just a login

The Cintana Alliance gives students at partner universities a real pathway to Arizona State — coursework, certifications, transfer routes. But that access is individual and scattered. Two problems compound:

Problem 01

No peer cohort

A student on this pathway at one university has no structured way to find, let alone learn alongside, a student on the same pathway somewhere else.

Problem 02

No applied practice

Modern development workflows, cloud and AI tooling, version-controlled project work — the things ASU coursework and any real technical role assume — are rarely built by a home campus curriculum alone.

The result

You arrive unprepared, alone

Left unaddressed, students prepare for ASU in isolation, with neither a peer group nor a structured skill-building track to get there.

02 — What we run

Six standing pillars

Run primarily online so members across three universities and every timetable can take part on equal footing.

Coding Foundations

The zero-barrier entry track. Programming basics, version control, everyday developer tooling and a first shipped website — no prior experience assumed, open to every major.

Skill Tracks

Multi-week cohort curricula in AI/ML, cloud infrastructure, full-stack development and data. Every cohort mixes members from different universities and ends in a reviewed, shipped project.

Certification & ASU pipeline

Guided cohorts working through ASU-accessible courses and certifications together, plus liaison office hours on transition and course catalogues.

Build Nights

Weekly drop-in working sessions on video. Bring what you're stuck on; peers and leads are on hand. Wherever you are.

Industry & alumni

Alumni now at ASU on what the pathway actually felt like, practitioner build talks, and an inter-university build challenge across all three campuses.

Transition & portfolio

One-to-one portfolio review ahead of transition or placement, plus structured technical interview practice — data structures, system design basics, live coding.

05 — Membership

Three tiers, each earned

Membership is open to students holding active Cintana Alliance / ASU pathway status at any partner university, and is renewed annually against that status.

Tier 01

Learner

Entry: active pathway status. Open registration, no application beyond that.

Full access to Skill Tracks, Build Nights and certification resources.

Tier 02

Builder

Entry: complete one Skill Track and ship two or more reviewed projects in a semester.

Official recognised title, priority access to certification seats and mentorship, and a vote in board elections.

Tier 03

Lead

Entry: nominated from top-performing Builders by the board.

Runs Skill Track cohorts and Build Nights. The board-track pathway.

07 — Questions

Before you enrol

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Coding Foundations exists precisely for people who don't — programming basics, version control and everyday tooling, with no prior experience assumed. Tech literacy is treated as valuable across every major, not only computing degrees, because ASU coursework increasingly assumes a baseline of it regardless of field.

Who can join?

Any student holding active Cintana Alliance / ASU pathway status at a partner university — currently REC, Shiv Nadar University and Anurag University. Membership is renewed annually against your pathway status, not against any one university's own registration.

Is this a club at one college?

No. The council is an official programme of the ASU Cintana Alliance. Recognition, funding and records sit with the Alliance rather than with any single partner university. Rajalakshmi Engineering College is credited as the founding chapter but holds no special authority over it.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Membership, Skill Tracks, Build Nights and Launch Day are free to students on the pathway.

How much time does it take?

Build Nights are weekly and drop-in. Skill Tracks run over several weeks with one cohort session a week plus your own project time. Monthly Tech Challenges are deliberately lightweight. You set the pace beyond the track you enrol in.

What happens to my data?

Enrolment details are stored securely, accessed only by the team that needs them, and are never shared outside the council without your explicit consent. You can ask us to delete your record at any time by emailing the council.
