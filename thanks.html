The ASU · Cintana pathway, in short

Why this council exists

The Cintana Alliance gives students at REC, Shiv Nadar University and Anurag University a real pathway to Arizona State — coursework, credit recognition, and eventually transfer or an MS route. What it doesn't come with is the room: a peer cohort on the same route, and the applied technical practice ASU coursework assumes but rarely gets built by a home-campus curriculum alone.

That's what the council adds — standing skill tracks, weekly build nights, a guided certification pipeline, and a portfolio you actually ship, alongside people making the same transition.
